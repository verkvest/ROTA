## Mixkit

`media/audio/sfx/mixkit-light-rain-looping-1249.wav` is "Light rain looping" from Mixkit (sound effect 1249), used as the rain ambience.

Mixkit Sound Effects are free to use in commercial and non-commercial end products, including video games, under the Mixkit Sound Effects Free License (https://mixkit.co/license/#sfxFree). Attribution is appreciated and is given here and in the in-game Licenses screen. The license does not allow redistributing the effect on its own, as stock audio, inside a tool or template, or as isolated source for someone else to reuse. It is included only as part of this game.

## Everything else in this repository

Original illustrations, tiles, character art, music (the in-game soundtrack), voice, and the remaining sound effects ship with the upstream project and are used under the MIT notice in `LICENSE`. No separate stock-media license was bundled with those files. If an asset later turns out to be under a license that does not allow this redistribution, it has to be removed.

People credited in the original game, and still credited in the Credits screen, include Harmony Honey Monroe, Guy Unger, Sam Murray, Raffaele Picca, neropatti, Cassidy Blaede, Will Thompson, and Micah Ilbery.
